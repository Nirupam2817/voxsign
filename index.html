📖 Sign Language Reference & Dictionary

🆘
Emergency / Help
Clenched fist with thumb up

Hold a fist with thumb pointing upwards or raise open hand. Triggers urgent emergency request.

💧
Water
ASL 'W' handshape

Extend index, middle, and ring fingers straight up in a 'W' shape. Speaks: "I need water, please".

🍽️
Food / Hungry
All fingertips pinched together

Bring all 5 fingertips together into a cup/pinched eating shape. Speaks: "I am hungry, I want food".

🚽
Restroom
ASL 'T' handshape

Thumb tucked under folded index finger. Speaks: "I need to use the restroom".

❤️
I Love You
Thumb + Index + Pinky up

Extend thumb, index, and pinky fingers while middle and ring fingers stay curled down.

👍
Yes / Agree
Thumbs Up

Close fist with thumb pointing straight upwards. Speaks: "Yes, I agree".

👎
No / Disagree
Thumbs Down

Close fist with thumb pointing downwards. Speaks: "No, I disagree".

👋
Hello / Greetings
Open hand fingers spread

Hold open hand with fingers spread comfortably. Speaks: "Hello! Good to see you".

✋
Stop / Wait
Flat palm facing forward

Flat hand with 5 fingers held together pointing straight up. Speaks: "Please stop and wait".

👌
OK / Good
Thumb + Index touching

Thumb and index fingertip touch in a circle; middle, ring, pinky extended up.

🤙
Call Someone / Phone
Thumb and Pinky out

Extend thumb and pinky wide (shaka), middle three fingers folded down.

✌️
Peace / Two
Index + Middle spread in V

Index and middle finger extended in a 'V' shape.

☝️
Look / Attention
Index finger up

Point index finger straight up. Speaks: "Please look here".

🩺
Doctor / Medical
Two fingers pointing down

Index and middle pointing down towards wrist. Speaks: "I need a doctor or medicine".

🅰️
Letter A

Fist with thumb resting alongside index finger.

🅱️
Letter B

4 fingers straight up together, thumb folded across palm.

🅲
Letter C

Curved fingers and thumb forming a 'C' shape.

🅳
Letter D

Index finger straight up, thumb touching middle, ring, pinky tips.

🅵
Letter F

Thumb and index touching in circle, other 3 fingers up.

🅸
Letter I

Pinky pointing straight up, all others curled into fist.

🅻
Letter L

Index up, thumb extended sideways forming an 'L'.

🆄
Letter U

Index and middle pointing up, touching side-by-side.

🆅
Letter V

Index and middle pointing up, spread apart in 'V'.

🆆
Letter W

Index, middle, and ring pointing up spread apart.

🆈
Letter Y

Thumb and pinky extended, other 3 folded down.

1️⃣
Number 1 to 5

1: Index up. 2: Index + Middle. 3: Thumb + Index + Middle. 4: Four fingers. 5: All five.
